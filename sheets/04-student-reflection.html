Honest Thinking Questions

Back of the sheet. Answer in full sentences — a few lines are plenty, and honest beats polished every time.

3. In my own words

3.1 If the AI had never existed, what would this assignment have cost you — more time, less quality, both, or nothing?

3.2 What's one thing the AI got wrong, left out, or pushed you toward that you ended up not using?

3.3 Where in your final work is there thinking that only you could have done — something a chatbot asked to do it cold would have produced differently?

3.4 Next major assignment, what will you do differently with AI — minus any guilt, as a practical choice?

3.5 Optional: anything you want me to know about your process, honestly?

For teachers: what to skim for

Read 3.3 first. If a student can't point to a single human-only choice in their work, that's the conversation to have — one-on-one, curious, not accusatory. Reflection sheets teach the habit; they don't have to convict anyone.
